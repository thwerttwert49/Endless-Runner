# CIS267_Platformer_MC
explanation of the game:

you have 3 lives

game generates new areas and deletes old areas as you move right

controls:
  double tap left or right to dash
  space to jump and you have 2 air jumps
  left mouse button to shoot
obstacles spawned during generation:
  obstacle 1: stickman enemy that walks into you and jumps if you are higher than it
  obstacle 2: jellyfish thing that can vary in size and health, it floats towards you
  obstacle 3: bricks that spawn and have 6 hp, sprite changes based on how damaged they are
score:
  enemies drop score collectibles
  you shoot enemies to destroy them
  score shows at top of screen when playing
  high score screen: not made
attribute collectibles:
  they spawn randomly during level generation
  green one heals you 1 health
  red one gives you larger, stronger and faster bullets
  orange one raises your fire rate
